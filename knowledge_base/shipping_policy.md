# Shipping Policy

## Standard Delivery
Standard orders are normally delivered within 3 to 7 business days after dispatch.

## Order Tracking
Customers can track their orders using the tracking information provided after dispatch.

## Delayed Deliveries
If an order is delayed beyond the estimated delivery date, customers should contact support with their order ID.

## Delivery Investigation
Support will review available tracking information and investigate reported delivery delays.

## Escalation
Orders delayed by more than 7 days beyond the estimated delivery date require human support review.

## Missing Packages
If tracking shows Delivered but the customer has not received the package, the issue must be escalated for investigation.

## Limitations
Support must not promise an exact delivery date, refund, or compensation without verified information and applicable policy authorization.
