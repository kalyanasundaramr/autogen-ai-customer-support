# Account and Login Support Policy

## Forgot Password
Customers who forget their password can use the Forgot Password option on the login page.

A password reset link will be sent to their registered email address if the account is eligible for password recovery.

## Password Reset
Customers should follow the instructions in the password reset email to create a new password.

Customers must never share passwords, OTPs, or password reset links with support agents.

## Login Problems
Customers experiencing login failures should:
1. Verify their registered email address.
2. Check their internet connection.
3. Use the Forgot Password option if necessary.
4. Contact support if the problem continues.

## Account Locked
Locked accounts require verification and human support review.

## Unauthorized Account Access
Suspected account hacking, unauthorized access, or suspicious login activity must be escalated immediately.

## Account Recovery
Account recovery requiring identity verification must be handled by authorized human support personnel.

## Limitations
AI support must not request passwords, OTPs, or other authentication secrets.

AI support must not claim to have unlocked an account or changed a password without verified system confirmation.
