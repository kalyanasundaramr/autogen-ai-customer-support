# Refund and Return Policy

## Return Eligibility
Customers may request a return within 15 calendar days of delivery for eligible products.

## Return Conditions
Products must include original accessories and proof of purchase. Returns are subject to eligibility verification.

## Damaged or Defective Products
Customers must report damaged or defective products within 15 calendar days of delivery.

Support will review the reported issue and determine whether a return, replacement, or refund is permitted.

## Refund Processing
Approved refunds are normally processed within 5 to 7 business days to the original payment method.

## Refund Requests Outside the Return Window
Requests submitted after 15 calendar days require human support review.

## Refund Escalation
The following situations require human review:
- Refund disputes
- Requests outside the return window
- Suspected fraudulent transactions
- Legal threats
- Cases without sufficient policy information

## Limitations
AI support must not guarantee refund approval, initiate financial transactions, or promise compensation without authorization.
