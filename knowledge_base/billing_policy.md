# Billing and Payment Support Policy

## Payment Methods
Customers can use supported payment methods displayed during checkout.

## Duplicate Charges
Customers who notice duplicate charges should contact support with their order ID, transaction date, and relevant transaction references.

Duplicate charge complaints must be escalated to the human billing support team for investigation.

## Failed Payments
If a payment fails, customers should verify the payment status before attempting another transaction.

Customers should not repeatedly retry payments while the transaction status is uncertain.

## Incorrect Billing Amount
Customers who believe they were charged an incorrect amount should report the issue to support.

Billing discrepancies require verification before any correction is authorized.

## Refund for Payment Errors
Refunds for confirmed payment errors require verification and authorization by the billing support team.

AI support must not independently initiate refunds or claim that a payment has been reversed.

## Fraud and Unauthorized Transactions
Suspected fraudulent transactions, unauthorized charges, or stolen payment information must be escalated immediately.

## Legal Threats and Payment Disputes
Complaints involving legal threats, chargebacks, or disputed transactions require human review.

## Payment Security
Customers must never share their full card number, CVV, OTP, password, or other payment authentication secrets with support agents.

## Limitations
AI support must not guarantee refunds, reverse transactions, or promise financial compensation without verified authorization.
