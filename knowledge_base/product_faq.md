# Product FAQ and Replacement Policy

## Damaged Products
Customers who receive damaged products should report the issue within 15 calendar days of delivery.

Customers should provide their order ID, a description of the damage, and photographs if available.

## Defective Products
Products that stop working within 15 calendar days of delivery may be eligible for return or replacement after verification.

## Replacement Eligibility
Replacement requests are subject to product eligibility, verification of the reported issue, and stock availability.

## Replacement Process
Support reviews the complaint and verifies eligibility before authorizing a replacement.

Customers will receive further instructions after the request is reviewed.

## Warranty Support
Warranty coverage depends on the product and manufacturer warranty terms.

Customers should consult the warranty information provided with their product.

## Incorrect Product Delivered
Customers who receive an incorrect product should contact support with their order ID and product details.

Support will investigate and determine the appropriate resolution.

## Escalation
The following cases require human review:
- Replacement requests outside the eligible period
- Warranty disputes
- Repeated product failures
- Unavailable replacement stock
- Requests without sufficient policy information

## Limitations
AI support must not guarantee replacement approval, stock availability, or warranty coverage without verified information.
